# Intervue AI — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Job seekers preparing for high-stakes interviews — often anxious, often practicing
alone at night or between work. They want to walk into the real room feeling ready.
The product is a candidate-facing AI mock-interview coach: create a role-specific
interview, answer on webcam, get exacting AI feedback, track progress.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **confident, warm, exacting.** The voice of a great coach who has seen
it work — direct, encouraging, never fluffy. Calm authority that lowers the
candidate's heart rate while raising their standards.

### Aesthetic Direction — "Editorial Coach"
A premium interview-prep book / a mentor's study. Print-editorial sensibility:
- **Theme:** single light theme, warm paper. No dark mode.
- **Type:** Hedvig Letters Serif for display marquee moments (sharp, contemporary
  serif). Hanken Grotesk for all body & UI (clean warm grotesque). No Inter, no
  Fraunces/Playfair/DM Serif (reflex defaults — banned).
- **Color (OKLCH):** warm paper `oklch(0.975 0.006 75)`, deep ink
  `oklch(0.23 0.014 55)`, single amber accent `oklch(0.62 0.15 55)`. Neutrals are
  tinted warm (toward the amber hue). Occasional deep warm-ink contrast band for
  CTAs/footer.
- **Layout:** asymmetric, editorial. Generous whitespace, real margins, body capped
  ~68ch. Borders and hairline rules over drop shadows. Numbered/lettered sections.
- **Motion:** restrained. One well-orchestrated staggered page-load reveal; quiet
  hover feedback. Ease-out, no bounce. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

### Design Principles
1. **Print, not panel.** Reach for rules, margins, and type hierarchy before cards
   and shadows. When a card is needed, it's a hairline-bordered sheet, not a floaty
   rounded box.
2. **One accent, used rarely.** Amber is the only color with a voice. 60% paper /
   30% ink-and-border / 10% amber. Overusing it kills it.
3. **The serif speaks once per view.** Hedvig display carries the single most
   important line on a page. Everything else is Hanken.
4. **Calm the nerves.** Roomy spacing, no aggressive motion, reassuring copy. The UI
   should never add stress to an already stressful moment.
5. **Never look AI-made.** No purple gradients, no gradient text, no glassmorphism,
   no border-left accent stripes, no identical icon-card grids.
